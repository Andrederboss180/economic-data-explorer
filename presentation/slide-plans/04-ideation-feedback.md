# 04. Ideation & Feedback

## Purpose

Show how AI supplied options and arguments while the human chose a feasible question.

## Main takeaway

A structured prompt supported topic selection, but the final scope followed human choices about feasibility and verification.

- AW-001 records the user's selection of the Explorer and narrowing to three countries, two indicators and basic statistics: [AI log](../../ai-workflow/log.md).
